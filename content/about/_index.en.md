<p>NAR-UQ is a textological study of the Wordry as a self-sufficient semantic system. Author — Serik Bolatjanovich Ryszhanov, NAR-UQ Foundation, Astana.</p>
<p><strong>Method.</strong> Isomorphic translation: one Arabic root — one Russian equivalent, strictly from the root, without tafsir meanings.</p>
<p><strong>Position.</strong> Impartial analyst, not a preacher. The Wordry is self-sufficient and needs no intermediaries.</p>
<p><strong>Goal.</strong> To show that everyone is capable of reading the Wordry independently, understanding any question and drawing their own conclusions — without intermediaries.</p>
