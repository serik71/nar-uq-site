Project Position

Trust in the Source

Only the Creator is objective, while humans are subjective. 1400 years of tradition have led away from revelation: 'scholars' criticize 'scholars', schools contradict schools — but all refer to the same base of traditions.

This project offers an alternative: understanding the Wordry from the Wordry itself. Without traditions. Without schools. Without intermediaries between man and the Word of the Creator.

Key Term
Wordry

Creativity expressed orally and in writing. Arabic قُرْآنٌ means 'recitation, declamation' and is the revelation of the Originator, recorded on parchment. The word 'wordry' encompasses both components: the spoken word and its written fixation. Not a calque, but meaning.

Name

What does NAR-UQ mean?

Қазақша
Нар-Ұқ

'Nar' — symbol of strength and power. 'Uq' in imperative — 'understand'. Read backwards as Quran.

Русский
Нарок

According to Dal's dictionary: code of rules and laws; vow, covenant; prescription, rule. Backwards — 'Koran'.

العربية
قُرْآنٌ

Arabic word. Translates as 'reading, recitation, declamation'. We use semantic translation — Wordry.
